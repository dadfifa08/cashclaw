# SOP and Work Instruction

Purpose:
Normalize validated reasoning into stepwise SOP-style deliverables.

Typical triggers:
procedure, SOP, work instruction, runbook

Primary outputs:
stepwise procedure, controls, validation steps

Preferred artifacts:
troubleshooting-procedure, service-report

Operating notes:
- Keep outputs deterministic where possible.
- Preserve evidence and provenance details for later revision and dataset use.
- Favor structured engineering detail over generic assistant phrasing.
- Keep user-facing responses concise, but let the artifact layer remain rich.
