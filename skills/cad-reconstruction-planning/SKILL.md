# CAD Reconstruction Planning

Purpose:
Plan the path from image/file evidence into reusable 2D or 3D engineering assets.

Typical triggers:
CAD, 3D, model, reconstruct, drawing

Primary outputs:
reconstruction plan, required evidence, modeling path

Preferred artifacts:
service-report, inspection-checklist

Operating notes:
- Keep outputs deterministic where possible.
- Preserve evidence and provenance details for later revision and dataset use.
- Favor structured engineering detail over generic assistant phrasing.
- Keep user-facing responses concise, but let the artifact layer remain rich.
