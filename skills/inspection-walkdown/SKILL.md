# Inspection Walkdown

Purpose:
Convert observed conditions and evidence into controlled inspection outputs.

Typical triggers:
inspection, checklist, visual review, audit

Primary outputs:
inspection checklist, pass-fail criteria, evidence plan

Preferred artifacts:
inspection-checklist, service-report

Operating notes:
- Keep outputs deterministic where possible.
- Preserve evidence and provenance details for later revision and dataset use.
- Favor structured engineering detail over generic assistant phrasing.
- Keep user-facing responses concise, but let the artifact layer remain rich.
