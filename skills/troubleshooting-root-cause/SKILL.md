# Troubleshooting Root Cause

Purpose:
Drive fault isolation, ranked root-cause hypotheses, and corrective actions.

Typical triggers:
errors, failures, root cause, diagnostics

Primary outputs:
ranked hypotheses, troubleshooting path, corrective actions

Preferred artifacts:
diagnostic-reasoning-log, troubleshooting-procedure, service-report

Operating notes:
- Keep outputs deterministic where possible.
- Preserve evidence and provenance details for later revision and dataset use.
- Favor structured engineering detail over generic assistant phrasing.
- Keep user-facing responses concise, but let the artifact layer remain rich.
