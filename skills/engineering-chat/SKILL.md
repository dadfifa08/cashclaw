# Engineering Chat

Purpose:
Keep the chat surface natural while preserving artifact-first engineering structure behind the scenes.

Typical triggers:
general questions, explainers, open-ended support

Primary outputs:
service report, diagnostic reasoning log, conversational projection

Preferred artifacts:
service-report, diagnostic-reasoning-log

Operating notes:
- Keep outputs deterministic where possible.
- Preserve evidence and provenance details for later revision and dataset use.
- Favor structured engineering detail over generic assistant phrasing.
- Keep user-facing responses concise, but let the artifact layer remain rich.
