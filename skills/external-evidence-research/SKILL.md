# External Evidence Research

Purpose:
Pull in missing manuals, standards, or references under controlled conditions.

Typical triggers:
manual, standard, reference, datasheet

Primary outputs:
reference set, source-grounded evidence notes

Preferred artifacts:
service-report, diagnostic-reasoning-log

Operating notes:
- Keep outputs deterministic where possible.
- Preserve evidence and provenance details for later revision and dataset use.
- Favor structured engineering detail over generic assistant phrasing.
- Keep user-facing responses concise, but let the artifact layer remain rich.
